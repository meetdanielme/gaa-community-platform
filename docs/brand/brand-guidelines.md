# SAINTS

## One club. Every generation.

An identity for the fictional Saints community club and its four Gaelic games codes. The compact shield and angular S give it a recognisable sporting silhouette without favouring one game. Navy leads; yellow adds energy. Red is a supporting accent.

## Logo files

- **saints-primary-logo.png**: horizontal shield and wordmark, 2172 × 724 px, transparent background. Use for presentation covers, website headers and club communications.
- **saints-compact-badge.png**: standalone shield, 1254 × 1254 px, transparent background. Use for avatars, app branding and small placements.
- **saints-brand-board.png**: visual identity overview and illustrative applications, 1536 × 1024 px.
- **saints-brand-board.pdf**: the same board on one landscape A3 PDF page.

The generated PNGs are digital artwork, not editable vector masters. The PDF contains the raster board. Reversed marks, clothing and app examples on the board illustrate the direction; they are not separate production assets or changes already applied to the Figma prototype.

## Logo use

- Place the navy/yellow logo on white or a very light neutral surface.
- Leave clear space on every side of at least one quarter of the shield's width, measured from the visible artwork.
- Start with a minimum visible width of 160 px for the full lockup and 24 px for the badge; prefer 32 px or more for the badge. Check the actual rendering at small sizes.
- Preserve proportions. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, crop, add shadows or replace the supplied lettering with a typed approximation.
- Keep the badge out of busy photography. Use a plain light panel when needed.
- Before embroidery, cutting or very large signage, prepare and approve a simplified vector version from the chosen artwork. No SVG is included in this kit.

### Readability

Blue on white has a calculated contrast ratio of 12.38:1; blue on yellow is 8.13:1. Black on white is 16.30:1. These work well for normal text. Red against white is 4.35:1, so avoid this pairing for small normal text. Use red as an accent alongside a clear dark-text label. Avoid yellow text on white. Detailed checks are in `contrast-checks.json`.

## Typography

Use **Inter** for new club communications and interface text: Bold for headings, Semi Bold for navigation and labels, Regular for body copy. It is free and open source under the SIL Open Font License: https://rsms.me/inter/.

Suggested starting scale: desktop title 40/48 px, section heading 28/36 px, card heading 22/30 px, body 16/24 px, labels 12–14 px Semi Bold. Keep the supplied wordmark as artwork. This guidance does not automatically replace the SF Pro/Inter typography already present in the prototype.

## Voice and applications

Write warmly and directly. Emphasise shared facilities, family participation and all four codes. Use “our club” and “our community”; avoid exaggerated claims or presenting sample events as real.

- **App:** compact badge in the navigation header; consistent blue navigation icons; yellow progress accents; generous white cards.
- **Social posts:** short blue or white headline, clear event details, one yellow rule and an occasional red accent. “MATCH DAY” on the board is a sample headline.
- **Clothing:** navy base, yellow trim and a small chest badge. The jersey is an illustrative concept.
- **Photography:** real community and Gaelic games settings, with source credits. Existing Unsplash photographs retain their own references and licences.
